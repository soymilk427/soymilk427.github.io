About關於我About me

我是 Jessica（黃熙芸／豆漿），目前從事社群內容創作與品牌商業影片拍攝，平時有較多旅遊、美食、生活及品牌產品介紹類型的出鏡經驗。

過去曾任職於台北市政府，工作中有大量正式場合的溝通、協調及對外應對經驗；也曾參與展場及品牌活動相關工作，具備現場與觀眾互動、產品介紹及品牌推廣的經驗。

此外，過去曾從事 Diageo Taiwan VIP／酒類品牌相關推廣工作，需要在活動及消費者接觸場合中介紹產品、品牌特色，並依照不同對象進行清楚且自然的溝通。

目前也持續進行短影音及商業合作拍攝，包含旅遊、美食、生活及品牌產品介紹等內容，熟悉面對鏡頭口述、產品資訊整理及依照腳本完成拍攝，也能配合現場拍攝團隊調整表達方式與拍攝節奏。

我擅長將較複雜的產品資訊整理成容易理解、自然且有記憶點的說法，個人鏡頭風格偏自然、親切，希望讓觀眾感覺是在「真的了解產品」，而不是單純背稿。對海外拍攝及跨國商業影片工作有興趣。

I'm Jessica Huang, a Taipei-based content creator working on social media content and branded commercial video, with most of my on-camera experience in travel, food, lifestyle and product introduction.

I previously worked at the Taipei City Government, where I handled a large volume of formal communication and coordination. I've also worked at trade shows and brand events, interacting with audiences, presenting products and promoting brands on site.

Earlier I worked on Diageo Taiwan VIP and spirits brand promotion, introducing products and brand stories to different audiences in a clear, natural way.

I'm comfortable speaking to camera, organising product information, shooting to a script, and adjusting delivery and pacing with a production team.

I'm good at turning complex product information into something easy to understand and remember. My on-camera style is natural and approachable: I want the audience to feel they actually understand the product, not that I'm reading a script. Open to overseas shoots and cross-border commercial work.
